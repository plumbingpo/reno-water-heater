Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater paired with the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Hot water temperature is a core issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature can stop service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and flow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to health inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we install overnight or before opening, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in tanks stored too cool, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Maintenance Programs

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Reno Water Heater program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve tests, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
