Keeping Your Sparks Water Heater Running Longer

Most early failures start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Flush the tank every year to clear sediment, have the anode rod inspected every few years, and operate the relief valve each year so it doesn't seize. Set the thermostat close to 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard to protect the heat exchanger.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular maintenance, a tank water heater typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts acting up, like discolored hot water, popping noises, or moisture under the tank, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new heater after a leak.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or a missing expansion tank, both modest fixes. The same drip ignored for months can wear out the valve and stress the tank until a seam gives way, and a small repair becomes a full replacement and a cleanup. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Sparks

Whatever type of water heater your Sparks home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks lose elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
